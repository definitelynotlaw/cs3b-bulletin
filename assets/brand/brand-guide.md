# Pilimon Bulletin brand guide

## Core palette

- Primary brand: `#183B70` — primary action color, navigation emphasis, active filters, and key focus states.
- Primary strong: `#102D56` — pressed or hovered primary states and dark surfaces.
- Secondary brand: `#C69842` — complementary accent for emphasis, metadata highlights, and light-theme warmth.
- Neutral ink: `#101828` — logo wordmark and strong text on light surfaces.
- Page background: `#F4F6F8` — light app background.
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` — cards and panels.
- Border: `#DCE5EE` — subtle separators and form lines.
- Text primary: `#1E2A36` — main interface text.
- Text secondary: `#586875` — metadata and tertiary content.
- Success: `#1E7B59` — positive or confirmation states.
- Warning: `#C69842` — pending or notable states.
- Error: `#B33F4B` — destructive actions.

## Dark-theme palette

- Primary brand: `#8BB3FF` — accent for interactive controls in dark mode.
- Primary strong: `#C9D9FF` — hover and selection highlight.
- Page background: `#101821` — dark app background.
- Surface: `#17222B` — elevated panels.
- Border: `rgba(163,181,203,0.2)` — subtle separators.
- Text primary: `#EDF2F8` — primary text in dark mode.
- Success: `#9DE0C2` — positive states.
- Error: `#FFB4BF` — destructive states.

## Logo family

- `assets/brand/p-symbol.svg` — standalone P symbol for compact marks and app branding.
- `assets/brand/favicon.svg` — favicon and browser-tab icon version of the symbol.
- `assets/brand/pilimon-bulletin-wordmark-light.svg` — light-theme horizontal wordmark.
- `assets/brand/pilimon-bulletin-wordmark-dark.svg` — dark-theme horizontal wordmark.
- `assets/brand/pilimon-bulletin-stacked-light.svg` — stacked wordmark for larger placements.
- `assets/brand/pilimon-bulletin-stacked-dark.svg` — stacked wordmark for dark surfaces.

The identity keeps the original Monochrome Version 1 logic: a strong geometric P, a subtle document-fold detail, and a clean uppercase wordmark with generous tracking.
